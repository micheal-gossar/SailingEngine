ASSET SUBSYSTEM INDEX
=====================

Authority and lifecycle
-----------------------

AssetId is a stable 128-bit logical ID. AssetRegistry is the immutable mapping
authority. AssetManager owns runtime request slots and state; typed Shader,
Material, Mesh, Texture, Audio, and data systems own constructed runtime
generations. Old generations remain retained through CPU readers and GPU
completion.

Dependency direction
--------------------

Cooker calls registered importer/processors; ModelImporter does not depend on
AssetCooker. Cooker emits manifests consumed by AssetRegistry. AssetManager
depends on VFS, Registry, JobSystem, and typed factories. Registry does not
depend on AssetManager. Hot reload prepares a new registry/artifact generation,
then separately asks affected runtime owners to construct and publish it at P13.
No runtime renderer dependency enters offline cooking.

Failure contract
----------------

Unknown/corrupt/missing/dependency-failed assets remain Failed with an Error.
Named presentation proxies are explicit policy and never look Ready under the
requested AssetId. See ..\ERROR_RESULT_POLICY.txt.
