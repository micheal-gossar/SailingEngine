WORLD SUBSYSTEM INDEX
=====================

Authorities
-----------

WorldStreamingManager owns the global observer set, desired cell plan, budgets,
and activation transaction. Participants own their resources. TerrainSystem
owns terrain chunk generations and exposes immutable height/collision/bathymetry
views. WeatherSystem owns deterministic weather snapshots. TimeOfDaySystem owns
calendar/solar time and scheduled event values.

Documents

09_weather_system.txt, 10_world_streaming_manager.txt,

Dependency and data flow
------------------------

At P4 TimeOfDay and Weather publish snapshots. Ocean, rendering, audio, and AI
consume them; Weather does not call those consumers. At P3 streaming commits
prepared participant operations. Terrain, NavigationGraph, OceanStreamingService,
audio zones, and gameplay cell content register participant interfaces and
return readiness/cost/rollback values. They do not maintain independent global
observer/budget policy.

SaveSystem snapshots participant-owned deltas and does not control residency.
Unknown terrain/bathymetry is explicit Unavailable, never zero height or
navigable water. See ..\FRAME_THREADING_MODEL.txt and
..\OWNERSHIP_LIFETIME_SHUTDOWN.txt.
