RENDERING SUBSYSTEM INDEX
=========================

Scope and ownership
-------------------

Rendering consumes immutable P12 snapshots and owns presentation-only state.
Renderer is the facade/composition owner for Vulkan-facing services and pass
providers. RenderGraph is the sole normal-frame synchronization planner.
RenderInterpolationSystem copies previous/current simulation values; it cannot
feed simulation.

Documents
---------

03_render_graph.txt and 70_renderer.txt define orchestration.
04_shader_pipeline_manager.txt, 41_material_system.txt, 42_mesh_system.txt, and
43_texture_system.txt own typed runtime/GPU generations.
23_camera_system.txt and 60_render_interpolation.txt extract views/world.
27_sky_atmosphere.txt, 28_particle_system.txt, 45_lighting_shadow.txt,
46_cloud_system.txt, and 47_storm_effects.txt provide passes.
56_graphics_quality.txt selects validated presentation budgets.

Dependency direction
--------------------

DescriptorAllocator is independent of shader policy; ShaderPipelineManager uses
it. Mesh assets reference material AssetIds but MeshSystem does not own
MaterialSystem. Draw packet assembly resolves both immutable handles. Lighting
consumes SkyLightingSnapshot; sky and lighting do not call one another.
GraphicsQualityManager publishes a reconfiguration plan; render systems transact their
own replacements and return results.

All GPU uses declare RenderGraph states and retire through GpuCompletionSet.
No render delta, readback, culling, visibility, particle, cloud, or audio-
listener interpolation can change authoritative state. See
..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt and ..\vulkan\INDEX.txt.
