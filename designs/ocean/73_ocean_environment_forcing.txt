OCEAN ENVIRONMENT FORCING CLASS DESIGN
======================================

Class
-----

    OceanEnvironmentForcing

Purpose
-------

Provide physically meaningful mean sea level, harmonic tides, depth-aware
currents, wind stress, pressure setup, river inflow, temperature/salinity, and
storm forcing to every ocean representation from one versioned source.

Public API
----------

    Result<void, OceanError> Configure(const OceanForcingDescription&);
    void SetWeatherGeneration(const WeatherOceanForcing&);
    void SetBoundaryTile(OceanBoundaryTileId, const BoundaryForcingTile&);
    void RemoveBoundaryTile(OceanBoundaryTileId);
    void Advance(OceanTick);
    ForcingSample Sample(const WorldPosition&, SampleTime) const;
    ForcingSnapshot AcquireSnapshot() const;

Key data structures
-------------------

TidalConstituent stores amplitude, phase lag, angular speed, optional nodal
correction, and spatial atlas channel for M2/S2/N2/K1/O1 and configured local
constituents. CurrentTile stores depth-layer velocities, sea-surface slope,
temperature, salinity, turbulence, river flux, and confidence. Weather forcing
stores 10 m wind, pressure, precipitation/evaporation, heat flux, fetch mask,
and storm surge boundary data. ForcingSample returns sea datum, Eulerian
current, Stokes drift separately, density, sound-speed inputs, and provenance.

Algorithms and models
---------------------

Evaluate tidal harmonics from double-precision epoch time using range-reduced
phase; do not integrate tide height per frame. Interpolate complex constituent
amplitudes spatially to avoid phase wrap. Compute equilibrium density from
temperature/salinity and a documented seawater equation approximation. Apply
wind stress using air/water density and drag coefficient; pressure gradients
and river boundary flux feed the coastal solver. Large-scale currents come from
cooked vector atlases or authored boundaries and are divergence-checked.

Stokes drift is derived from the deep directional spectrum and exposed
separately so vessel hydrodynamics and ecosystem transport can choose whether
to include it. Tide, storm surge, wave setup, and mean dynamic topography have
separate fields and are combined exactly once by OceanQueryService.

Wave/current coupling uses intrinsic frequency sigma and the two absolute
branches omegaPlusMinus=k dot U +/- sigma. Uniform current advects the real
surface by a common signed-k phase and shifts group velocity by U; it must not
break Fourier conjugate symmetry. Slowly varying current gradients refract
action directions. Opposing-current blocking is detected when the relevant
absolute group velocity approaches zero and transfers unresolved energy to
dissipation/breaking policy. EnvironmentForcing supplies U and gradients but
DeepWaterSpectralOcean/CoastalOceanSolver own the wave solve, so current
advection is not applied twice.

Boundary values use SI and the conventions in
87_ocean_conventions_invariants.txt: eta/zb metres in the world datum, current
metres/second toward motion, pressure Pa, stress N/m2, temperature K, salinity
g/kg, and positive volume/momentum flux into the receiving coastal tile.

Update rates, threading, and GPU ownership
-----------------------------------------

Weather inputs latch at 1 Hz or on generation change. Tide/current snapshots
publish at 2 Hz and are analytically sampled between publications. Boundary
tiles stream on jobs. The CPU snapshot is authoritative. GPU atlases are
read-only packed copies for coastal/render/acoustic compute and are uploaded by
the render resource owner through RenderGraph transfer passes; they never feed
authoritative CPU state through readback.

Lifecycle and dependencies
--------------------------

Configure after WeatherSystem, SimulationClock, TerrainSystem bathymetry datum,
and OceanStreamingService. Boundary tiles may arrive before activation but
become visible only at a tick boundary. On unload, immutable snapshots keep
pages alive until readers retire. Save stores epoch and dynamic boundary
overrides, not analytically reconstructible harmonic samples.

See ..\world\09_weather_system.txt, ..\world\26_terrain_system.txt,
..\core\37_simulation_clock.txt, 74_deep_water_spectral_ocean.txt,
75_coastal_ocean_solver.txt, and 84_underwater_acoustics.txt.

Failure and error behavior
--------------------------

Errors include InvalidConstituent, DatumMismatch, MissingBoundary,
NonDivergenceFreeCurrent, InvalidThermohalineValue, StaleWeatherGeneration, and
NonFiniteForcing. Missing optional atlas data returns configured climatology
with low confidence. Missing required coastal boundary data blocks that coastal
tile; it cannot silently use zero tide/current. Out-of-range environmental
values are rejected at cook/load rather than clamped invisibly at runtime.

Deterministic and testing strategy
----------------------------------

Constituent order is canonical, epoch conversion uses a tested UTC/astronomical
time policy, interpolation and reductions have stable order, and stochastic
weather perturbations use cell/tick counter RNG. Tests cover tide-gauge golden
curves, phase interpolation across 360 degrees, datum conversion, current-tile
seams, mass-flux balance, density/sound-speed reference tables, save/restore,
weather transitions, intrinsic/absolute frequency under uniform current,
opposing-current blocking policy, current-gradient refraction, and large epoch
times.

Performance and scalability
---------------------------

Use sparse clipmapped forcing tiles, quantized cooked atlases with documented
error, SoA constituent coefficients, and SIMD batch evaluation. Cache only by
cell plus time bucket and include generation in keys. Quality levels may remove
vertical current layers or fine turbulence, but never change tide datum,
low-frequency current, or authoritative density seen by vessel physics.

Integration points
------------------

DeepWaterSpectralOcean consumes wind/fetch/stability targets. CoastalOceanSolver
consumes sea-level and momentum/river boundaries. OceanQueryService combines
forcing fields. MarineEcosystemManager uses temperature, salinity, flow, and
productivity drivers. UnderwaterAcoustics uses thermohaline profiles. Surface
and underwater renderers consume the packed visual snapshot.

Engine-wide integration contract
--------------------------------

Normative forcing-state owner. Weather, time, terrain, and streaming are
immutable input generations/data-flow dependencies; this module never calls
their owners. Publication is P9 for N+1, sampling is generation-bound, and all
failures use ..\ERROR_RESULT_POLICY.txt. Root axes/SI/time/seeds are defined by
..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt and tightened by file 87.
