DEEP WATER SPECTRAL OCEAN CLASS DESIGN
======================================

Class
-----

    DeepWaterSpectralOcean

Purpose
-------

Model deep and intermediate offshore wind waves with deterministic directional
spectra, physically consistent dispersion, cascaded inverse FFT rendering, and
a matched authoritative low-mode CPU representation.

Public API
----------

    Result<SpectrumId, OceanError> CreateSpectrum(
        const DirectionalSpectrumDescription&);
    void SetForcingTarget(SpectrumId, const SpectrumForcingTarget&);
    void AdvanceEpoch(OceanTick);
    DeepWaveCpuView AcquireCpuView() const;
    DeepWaveGpuWork BuildGpuWork(const OceanRenderSnapshot&) const;
    SpectrumDiagnostics Diagnostics(SpectrumId) const;

Key data structures
-------------------

DirectionalSpectrumDescription contains physical patch lengths, NxN resolution,
gravity, surface tension option, depth regime, energy model, directional
spreading, fetch, swell partitions, damping, seed, and transition half-life.
SpectralMode stores integer wave index, double world-space wave vector, angular
frequency, complex H0 pair, and partition id. CpuModeSet contains every mode in
the fixed vessel-response band plus a deterministic energy-ranked residual
approximation and a certified displacement/slope error bound. GpuCascade contains H0,
time-domain complex fields, displacement/slope/Jacobian outputs, mip chain,
phase origin, and timeline generation.

Algorithms and models
---------------------

Generate variance from JONSWAP for fetch-limited seas and configurable
Pierson-Moskowitz/swell partitions; a Phillips-style directional tail may fill
unresolved high frequencies but cannot define the whole sea. Convert S(omega,
theta) to Cartesian spectral density with the correct Jacobian. Directional
spreading is normalized and may be bimodal around changing winds. Counter RNG
generates independent complex Gaussian H0(k) values for both signed members of
each non-self k/-k pair, keyed so traversal/thread order cannot change them.
H0(-k) is not forced to conj(H0(k)); that would collapse the stationary random
sea into temporally locked standing phases. The coupled time evolution, not H0
alone, enforces a real surface. DC is zero unless a named mean mode exists;
self-conjugate Nyquist bins are evolved as real coefficients. Initialization
validates finite non-negative density, H0 statistics, evolved conjugate
symmetry, variance/Parseval agreement, peak energy, and no duplicate energy in
cascade overlap before publication.

Dispersion uses sigma^2=(g*k*tanh(k*h)) plus capillary term where enabled.
Without current, evaluate
H(k,t)=H0(k)e^(i*sigma*t)+conj(H0(-k))e^(-i*sigma*t), and form/check the paired
bin so H(-k,t)=conj(H(k,t)). Range reduce time against an epoch. Compute
horizontal choppy displacement, slope, vertical
velocity, orbital velocity, and displacement Jacobian in frequency space.
Stockham inverse FFTs transform all fields; normalization and sign conventions
are fixed by conformance vectors. Cascades cover near capillary/gravity detail,
mid sea, and long swell with energy-preserving overlap windows.

All cascades share the same global epoch, counter-RNG phase function, signed
world wave vector, and double phase origin. A physical k band belongs to one
partition-of-unity overlap pair; both cascades evaluate the identical global
phase before weighted synthesis. Clipmap recentering changes sample coordinates,
not H0 or phase. Cascade length/resolution changes create a cross-faded spectral
generation whose common modes copy exact complex coefficients; new modes draw
from their global keys. This prevents independent tiles/cascades from swimming.

For a uniform current U, multiply the paired intrinsic solution by the common
advection phase exp(-i*k dot U*t) under file 87's Fourier convention; the two
absolute branches are k dot U +/- sigma and conjugate symmetry remains exact.
Orbital kinematics use intrinsic sigma. Spatially varying current is accepted
only when variation across a patch is below the WKB threshold; otherwise wave
action is handed to CoastalOceanSolver. Opposing-current blocking, a reversed
relevant absolute group velocity, or steep gradients trigger explicit transfer/
dissipation, not clamped phase.

The authoritative CPU cutoff is derived at world creation from the smallest
supported hull waterline/sample spacing and maximum sea state, then persisted
in OceanEpoch. Graphics quality never changes it. Wavelengths too short for the
declared vessel/hydrodynamic resolution contribute their integrated slope
variance to surface roughness and spray, not a hidden gameplay displacement.
The retained residual approximation must remain below certified height, slope,
and orbital-velocity error bounds at query points.

Weather changes relax spectral energy/partition direction over physical time;
they do not regenerate random phases. New partitions cross-fade in complex
amplitude while conserving target variance. A depth/shore mask transfers energy
to CoastalOceanSolver instead of multiplying height to zero at land.

Update rates, threading, and GPU ownership
-----------------------------------------

CPU low modes are analytic at each 60 Hz authoritative query tick; coefficient
targets update at 1 Hz. GPU inverse FFT runs per render frame, preferably async
compute. DeepWaterSpectralOcean owns persistent spectrum/cascade buffers;
RenderGraph owns transient ping-pong aliases and barriers. Compute completion
signals graphics sampling through graph edges. No GPU result is read back for
vessel physics.

Lifecycle and dependencies
--------------------------

Create after OceanEnvironmentForcing and the epoch. Cook/validate spectrum
descriptions before GPU allocation. Resize or quality change allocates a new
cascade generation, warms it, then atomically swaps at a frame boundary; old
resources retire through the central queue after their GpuCompletionSet. On
device recreation, rebuild H0 exactly from
seed/version. See ..\rendering\03_render_graph.txt,
..\vulkan\19_command_scheduler.txt, ..\vulkan\02_gpu_resource_manager.txt,
73_ocean_environment_forcing.txt, and 76_ocean_query_service.txt.

Failure and error behavior
--------------------------

Errors include InvalidSpectrum, NonPowerOfTwoResolution, InvalidFetch,
DispersionDomainError, UnsupportedFftSize, GpuAllocationFailed,
SpectrumGenerationMismatch, ConjugateSymmetryViolation,
CascadePhaseMismatch, CurrentWkbViolation, and NonFiniteSpectrum. Reject negative energy and
non-normalized spreading. If a visual cascade fails, omit only that cascade and
renormalize overlap; authoritative CPU modes persist. Never replace failed FFT
state with unrelated procedural sine waves.

Deterministic and testing strategy
----------------------------------

Spectrum generation is versioned and independent of thread order. Tests verify
Hermitian symmetry, real inverse output, Parseval energy, target significant
wave height, peak period/direction, dispersion values, zero-wind swell,
cross-cascade overlap, weather transition continuity, phase at huge world/time
coordinates, and scalar/SIMD agreement. Small FFT golden grids run on CPU and
GPU; selected world points compare displacement, derivatives, and velocity to a
tolerance owned by OceanValidationAndQuality.
Additional tests prove H0 signed-pair independence/statistics, initialize every
DC/Nyquist case, evolve H(k,t)=conj(H(-k,t)) over long epochs, recenter/resize
cascades without phase jump, verify overlap energy/phase, and compare uniform-
current advection plus both Doppler branches/group velocities under following
and opposing currents.

Performance and scalability
---------------------------

Use fused multi-field FFT passes, subgroup operations where measured, packed
complex formats only after error validation, async compute, and update-rate
reduction for far cascades. CPU modes are SoA and SIMD batch evaluated. Quality
scales cascade count/resolution/high-frequency cap; patch length, physics cutoff,
and retained authoritative modes remain stable to prevent buoyancy changes. Budget
metrics include FFT milliseconds, bandwidth, transient bytes, and queue overlap.

Integration points
------------------

OceanEnvironmentForcing supplies wind/fetch/swell targets. CoastalOceanSolver
receives boundary energy/direction/phase bands. OceanQueryService evaluates CPU
modes and blending. OceanSurfaceRenderer samples GPU displacement/slope/
Jacobian. OceanEffectsSystem derives crest candidates. HydrodynamicsSystem consumes
wave orbital velocity through the query service, never directly.

Engine-wide integration contract
--------------------------------

Normative owner of deep spectrum CPU/GPU generations. It allocates no timeline
values and owns no deferred-deletion queue: RenderGraph declares uses,
CommandScheduler returns queue-qualified points, and FrameManager retires the
module's payload. Authoritative CPU modes advance from TickId/epoch only; render
FFT/residuals cannot feed simulation. See ..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt and
..\vulkan\INDEX.txt.
