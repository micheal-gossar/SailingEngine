MARINE AGENT SIMULATION CLASS DESIGN
====================================

Class
-----

    MarineAgentSimulation

Purpose
-------

Simulate nearby schooling fish, predators, whales, dolphins, turtles and
relevant seabirds as data-oriented full agents with physically bounded motion,
environmental sensing, group behavior, vessel response, and scalable animation.

Public API
----------

    Result<void, MarineAgentError> Promote(const AgentPromotionBatch&);
    AgentDemotionBatch Demote(span<const MarineAgentId>);
    void SetObservers(span<const EcosystemObserver>);
    void FixedUpdate(OceanTick);
    MarineAgentRenderPacket BuildRenderPacket(const ViewFamily&) const;
    void QueueStimuli(const MarineStimulusBatch&);
    MarineAgentDiagnostics Diagnostics() const;

Key data structures
-------------------

Agent SoA components include stable id/generation, species/stage, cell/local
double position, orientation, linear/angular velocity, energy/health, locomotion
limits, behavior state/timers, group/pod id, target, perception LOD, animation
phase, acoustic cooldown, and cohort reservation. SchoolBlock stores member
ranges, centroid/velocity covariance, leader/goal, split/merge state, and threat.
PerceptionGrid is a cell-relative spatial hash with deterministic sorted member
lists. MarineStimulus stores source class, position, spectrum/intensity,
visibility, duration, and tick.

Algorithms and models
---------------------

Fish schools combine separation, alignment, cohesion, flow following, obstacle/
seabed avoidance, habitat attraction, predator evasion, and prey pursuit.
Steering accelerations are prioritized and clamped by species turn rate, burst
speed, drag, and energy; positions are not teleported to satisfy boids. Large
schools use hierarchical blocks: near members update individually, middle
blocks update representative distributions, and far groups remain ecosystem
cohorts. Split/merge conserves membership and stable IDs.

Whales/dolphins use pod-level goals, dive/breathe cycles, buoyancy/locomotion
states, calf cohesion, acoustic communication, and surface-clearance checks.
Seabirds use air flight dynamics from wind, water landing/takeoff, flock goals,
and prey-density cues; they query the surface rather than a flat plane.
Predator/prey encounters use bounded detection, pursuit, escape, energy, and
non-graphic resolve states. No gore/dismemberment representation is exposed.

Vessel behavior samples hull velocity/noise/wake and species sensitivity:
avoidance predicts closest approach, respects maneuver limits, and has panic
cooldown. Curious species may approach only under configured safe distance/
speed rules. Agents collide with coarse terrain/hull avoidance volumes, not
per-triangle physics. Flow, wave orbital velocity, turbidity, and light affect
sensing and locomotion without forcing animation-only movement.

Tier ownership is explicit:
    Tier 0 full agent: stable identity, individual perception/behavior/motion.
    Tier 1 school/pod block: member identity range plus moments and group goal.
    Tier 2 cell cohort: abundance/biomass/distribution in EcosystemManager.
Only one tier owns an individual/reservation at a tick boundary. Promotion and
demotion use sampled moment-preserving transforms, hysteresis, and a ledger.
Migration goals originate from EcosystemManager. Full agents may cross cells
locally; boundary handoff transfers their reservation atomically. Long-distance
migration demotes to a transit cohort instead of simulating offscreen agents.

Vessel avoidance predicts closest point/time of approach from relative hull and
agent motion, expands the hull by species reaction distance plus uncertainty,
and chooses a feasible acceleration/depth/turn response. Hull, propulsor, wake,
surface, terrain, and acoustic stimuli have priorities and refractory times so
agents do not oscillate between contradictory steering goals. Animation reads
resulting physical speed/curvature; root motion never drives authoritative
position.

Animation uses phase-continuous procedural swim/fin/tail parameters plus cooked
skeleton clips. Motion speed/turn drives phase and body bend. Renderer LOD
transitions full skinned mesh to reduced skeleton, vertex animation texture,
impostor/group density, then statistical cohort with dithered hysteresis.

Update rates, threading, and GPU ownership
-----------------------------------------

Critical nearby mammals/predators update behavior at 30 Hz and locomotion at
60 Hz; fish individuals at 15-30 Hz with interpolated motion; block perception
at 5-15 Hz; seabird flight at 30 Hz. Jobs process stable species/group chunks,
then a canonical interaction reduction resolves shared events. CPU owns agent
identity, behavior, movement, and ecosystem outcomes. GPU owns skinning, VAT,
instance culling, animation interpolation, and indirect draws only.

Lifecycle and dependencies
--------------------------

Agents are created only by ecosystem promotion or explicit validated scenario
spawns, carrying a cohort reservation. EntityRegistry handles gameplay-facing
generational handles while SoA storage remains internally compact. Before cell
unload or distance demotion, settle stimuli/interactions and return state to
MarineEcosystemManager. Persistent named animals retain identity records.
Device loss affects rendering only.

Failure and error behavior
--------------------------

Errors include StaleAgent, MissingCohortReservation, SpeciesDataInvalid,
AgentCapacity, PerceptionOverflow, InvalidStimulus, NavigationUnavailable,
DemotionLedgerMismatch, TierOwnershipConflict, CellHandoffFailed, and
AvoidanceInfeasible. Capacity failure leaves animals statistical. Perception
overflow deterministically retains safety-critical/vessel/predator stimuli and
nearest neighbors. Invalid navigation makes the agent slow/avoid and seek a
valid volume; it never teleports through terrain. Non-finite state restores the
agent's last valid snapshot and schedules safe demotion.

Deterministic and testing strategy
----------------------------------

Stable IDs, group ordering, counter RNG, fixed ticks, and fixed reduction trees
define replay. Tests cover school cohesion without collapse, bounded acceleration,
split/merge membership, obstacle and shoreline avoidance, current following,
predator/prey energy transfer, whale dive/breathe cycle, dolphin pod cohesion,
seabird landing on waves, vessel closest-approach avoidance, wake response,
promotion/demotion round trip, animation phase across LOD, origin shift,
thread-count replay, and save/restore of named animals.
Tier tests sweep observer distance/camera cuts and require exact membership,
bounded moment error, no rapid churn, local/transit migration equivalence, no
hull penetration in supported scenarios, avoidance time-to-collision margin,
and root-motion-independent replay.

Performance and scalability
---------------------------

Use archetype SoA, spatial hashes, group-level perception, time slicing,
species-specific update tiers, SIMD steering, batched ocean queries, GPU
skinning/VAT and indirect instancing. Enforce budgets independently for agents,
perception pairs, skeleton bones, acoustic events, and draws. Degrade small fish
to group blocks/cohorts before mammals or safety behavior. Profile agents/tier,
neighbor pairs, query samples, behavior/locomotion time, draw instances, bone
work, promotion churn, and missed deadlines.

Integration points
------------------

Ecosystem owns population truth. OceanQueryService supplies surface/depth/flow.
Terrain/habitats supply signed navigation volumes and shelter/feeding fields.
Vessels/wakes/audio create stimuli. UnderwaterAcoustics routes calls/noise and
returns perceived acoustic stimuli. Animation/Renderer consume render packets.
Save/streaming coordinate demotion and named identity state.

Engine-wide integration contract
--------------------------------

Normative promoted-agent runtime owner at P10. Ecosystem reservation is the
exclusive authority token; EntityRegistry mapping is gameplay identity, and
PersistentEntityId is used only for saved named animals. Inputs are immutable
ocean/habitat/vessel/acoustic snapshots; outputs are demotion ledgers, events,
and render packets. Root-motion/render/GPU values never drive position.
WorldSeed128 counter streams and stable reductions define replay. See
..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt and ..\FRAME_THREADING_MODEL.txt.
