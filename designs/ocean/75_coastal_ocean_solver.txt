COASTAL OCEAN SOLVER CLASS DESIGN
=================================

Class
-----

    CoastalOceanSolver

Purpose
-------

Transform offshore wave/current/tide forcing over bathymetry into refracted,
shoaled, breaking nearshore waves and conservative shallow-water flow, including
wetting/drying, setup, surf currents, harbor response, and long vessel waves.

Public API
----------

    Result<CoastalTileId, OceanError> ActivateTile(
        const CoastalTileDescription&, BathymetryGeneration);
    void DeactivateTile(CoastalTileId);
    void SetBoundary(CoastalTileId, const CoastalBoundaryState&);
    void QueueSource(CoastalTileId, const ConservativeWaterSource&);
    void Advance(OceanTick, Duration budget);
    CoastalQueryView AcquireQueryView() const;
    CoastalRenderView AcquireRenderView() const;

Key data structures
-------------------

BathymetryTile stores signed double datum elevation, roughness, porosity,
bottom type, structure boundaries, uncertainty, and terrain generation.
HydroCell stores water depth h and conserved momenta hu/hv. WaveActionBand
stores action density N=E/omega_intrinsic by frequency/direction, phase anchor,
group velocity, dissipation, and breaking fraction. CoastalWaveComponent stores
range-reduced eikonal phase, local wave vector, zero-mean elevation/velocity
amplitude, group phase id, and breaking limiter. CoastalTileState contains
ghost borders, wet/dry flags, CFL accumulator, residual mass/momentum, solver
tier, and generation. ConservativeWaterSource carries volume and momentum with
spatial kernel and reaction owner.

Algorithms and models
---------------------

Boundary conditions are typed, never inferred from a missing neighbor:
    OffshoreOpen uses characteristic/radiation conditions for mean eta/normal
        transport plus incoming directional wave components/action.
    RiverDischarge prescribes integrated m3/s and momentum with stage fallback.
    SolidWall reflects normal momentum with named free-slip/no-slip roughness.
    PorousStructure/Weir applies calibrated transmission, overtopping, and loss.
    NestedTile exchanges one canonical face flux with restriction/prolongation.
    TemporaryClosed is allowed only during explicit degraded recovery.
Ghost cells are filled once per substep from time-interpolated boundary
snapshots. Corner priority and open-boundary outgoing characteristics are
specified so tide/current/waves are not reflected or prescribed twice.
For different LOD/time-step neighbors, fine faces accumulate space-time
integrated mass/momentum in a reflux register. At their synchronization tick,
the summed fine flux replaces the coarse face flux; the coarse and fine tiles
never each apply an independently solved exchange. Ghost prolongation is
bounded and well-balanced, and a tile cannot publish while its reflux register
for that generation is unsettled.

Intermediate-depth wave action transport follows rays/group velocity over
current and bathymetry. Dispersion-derived wave number bends direction by
Snell/eikonal gradients; action conservation produces shoaling. Bottom friction
and depth-limited breaking use calibrated physical closures (for example
Battjes-Janssen bulk dissipation), reporting dissipated energy to foam/spray and
wave setup rather than deleting it invisibly.

Wave action alone has no instantaneous surface phase. Therefore a bounded set
of CoastalWaveComponents transports eikonal phase from the offshore spectral
boundary while amplitude comes from action density. Components reconstruct
zero-mean incident-wave elevation and orbital velocity for authoritative vessel
queries. Their depth-limited breaker removes coherent component energy and
deposits its mean momentum/setup into HydroCell state. Weakly dispersive tiles
solve equivalent phase-resolved waves directly and do not add components again.

Mean flow uses finite-volume nonlinear shallow-water equations. Hydrostatic
reconstruction keeps lake-at-rest balance; an HLLC-class positivity-preserving
flux handles shocks/bores; slope limiting, semi-implicit bottom drag, open/wall/
porous boundaries, and wet/dry hysteresis prevent negative depth. CFL controls
substeps. Selected surf/harbor tiles may use a validated weakly dispersive
Boussinesq correction; if unavailable they retain conservative SWE, not a
cosmetic wave texture.

Wetting/drying uses two declared SI thresholds hDry<hWet. A dry cell has h=0
and zero momentum; a face can wet it only through a positivity-preserving
Riemann flux with enough free-surface head. Hydrostatic reconstruction clips
reconstructed face depth, not conserved cell volume. Semi-implicit friction
cannot reverse momentum in one substep. Residual roundoff volume is held in a
signed tile ledger and reintroduced conservatively, never discarded per cell.

At the offshore overlap, project DeepWaterSpectralOcean energy into action
bands and phase-coherent boundary components with stable phase ids. Blend by wave energy/action and
group flux. OceanQueryService chooses deep versus coastal displacement through
a partition-of-unity mask; it never adds both full amplitudes. Neighbor tiles
exchange conservative flux once using a canonical edge owner.

Deep/coastal exchange owns a physical overlap control band. Mean tide/current
volume and momentum cross its control faces through characteristic fluxes.
Zero-mean wind-wave components carry action/energy/radiation stress but no net
water volume. Refraction/shoaling/breaking changes are entered in an exchange
ledger for action, energy, momentum, setup, and dissipation. Deep and coastal
blend windows share the same world phase and sum to one. Each tick verifies
that incoming minus outgoing/storage/dissipation stays within file 90
tolerances; failed exchange freezes the boundary generation.

Update rates, threading, and GPU ownership
-----------------------------------------

Boundary forcing publishes at 2 Hz. Active tiles advance at 10-30 Hz with
deterministic CFL substeps; vessel-adjacent high-interest tiles may run 60 Hz.
CPU finite volume is authoritative for waterline, vessel queries, flooding, and
save state. Tile jobs use colored/Morton batches and join at flux exchange.
Optional GPU coastal grids refine visuals from the same boundary/state but
cannot affect gameplay. GPU render copies are owned by this module and consumed
read-only after transfer/compute barriers.

Lifecycle and dependencies
--------------------------

OceanStreamingService requests a bathymetry tile. TerrainSystem publishes a
matching immutable generation and vertical datum. Solver allocates state,
initializes a balanced surface from tide/current, exchanges ready ghost borders,
then activates at a tick boundary. Deactivation seals edges, serializes dynamic
residuals, and retires readers. Terrain edits require conservative remapping to
a new generation before swap.

Failure and error behavior
--------------------------

Errors include DatumMismatch, MissingBathymetry, StaleBathymetry,
UnstableBoundary, CflBudgetExceeded, NegativeDepth, NonFiniteFlux,
NeighborGenerationMismatch, BoundaryCharacteristicFailure,
UnsettledRefluxRegister, ExchangeLedgerViolation, and ConservationViolation. If budget cannot meet
CFL, clamp the tick's external forcing and mark degraded rather than taking an
unstable large step. A failed tile rolls back to its last valid snapshot,
closes flux boundaries consistently, and emits a visible/query confidence flag.
Deep fallback is prohibited where the bathymetry says land or shallow water.

Deterministic and testing strategy
----------------------------------

Canonical edge ownership, stable tile ordering, rational substep buckets,
fixed limiter choices, and deterministic source accumulation define replay.
Tests include lake at rest over uneven bed, dam break, wet/dry beach run-up,
mass/momentum residual bounds, solitary-wave speed, current advection, Snell
refraction over a slope, Green-law shoaling range, breaker onset/dissipation,
harbor resonance, neighbor seams, tide boundary, terrain-generation swap,
vessel source/reaction balance, and save/load continuation.
The boundary matrix additionally tests still-water open boundaries, outgoing
wave non-reflection, tide plus current plus incoming wave, river flux integral,
wall/porous/overtopping behavior, hDry/hWet chatter, dry momentum zeroing,
mixed-LOD canonical flux, and deep/coastal volume/action/energy closure.
Mixed-LOD tests subcycle adversarial wet/dry bores across every edge/corner and
require exact paired reflux accounting independent of tile job order.

Performance and scalability
---------------------------

Only wet/boundary/forced tiles run. Use SoA grids, halo slabs, SIMD Riemann
solves, adaptive tile tiers, coarser action bands offshore, and sleeping harbor
tiles. Quality reduces direction/frequency bands, dispersive correction region,
or visual refinement before reducing authoritative waterline resolution around
vessels. Track cell-updates/s, substeps, wet fraction, flux residual, memory,
queue delay, and source count per tile.

Integration points
------------------

TerrainSystem supplies bathymetry/materials. Environment forcing supplies tide,
currents, rivers, and wind/pressure. Deep spectral ocean supplies offshore wave
action. Interaction system supplies volume/momentum sources and receives
reaction. Query service consumes immutable coastal state. Surface/effects/media
renderers consume render fields and breaker dissipation. Ecosystem/habitats use
depth, current, turbidity, exposure, and wet/dry state.

Engine-wide integration contract
--------------------------------

Normative owner of coastal conserved state. P9 advances immutable N inputs to
N+1; jobs write private tiles and join before canonical reflux/publication.
WorldStreamingManager owns admission while OceanStreamingService coordinates
the participant transaction; neither owns tile resources. CPU state is
authoritative and all optional GPU copies declare RenderGraph uses and central
GpuCompletionSet retirement. See ..\FRAME_THREADING_MODEL.txt,
..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt, and 89_bathymetry_pipeline.txt.
