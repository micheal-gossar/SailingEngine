OCEAN EFFECTS SYSTEM CLASS DESIGN
=================================

Class
-----

    OceanEffectsSystem

Purpose
-------

Convert resolved wave breaking, crest compression, vessel turbulence, impacts,
rain, and aeration into persistent foam, whitewater, spray, bubbles, mist, and
suspended sediment without inventing a second unrelated wave simulation.

Public API
----------

    void SetQuality(const OceanEffectsQuality&);
    void Ingest(const OceanEffectsEventBatch&);
    void AddSimulationPasses(RenderGraphBuilder&, const OceanRenderSnapshot&);
    void AddRenderPasses(RenderGraphBuilder&, const ViewFamily&);
    EffectsCpuSummary AcquireAudioSummary() const;
    OceanEffectsStatistics Statistics() const;

Key data structures
-------------------

BreakerEvent contains cell/local position, line direction, dissipated energy,
crest velocity, depth, salinity, and source generation. AerationEvent records
entrained air volume, momentum, size spectrum, and source. FoamTile stores
coverage, thickness proxy, age, velocity, and source provenance. SprayParticle
is a GPU SoA entry with position split, velocity, radius, lifetime, and seed.
BubbleVolume stores concentration/size bands, rise velocity, turbulence, and
optical/acoustic coupling. EffectLedger sums input, visible, dissipated, and
culled energy/air-volume proxies.

Algorithms and models
---------------------

Wave Jacobian compression proposes deep crest candidates; CoastalOceanSolver's
breaking dissipation is authoritative near shore; vessel wakes provide
turbulent/aeration sources. Threshold/hysteresis converts dissipated energy to
whitewater coverage. Foam advects with surface current plus Stokes drift,
diffuses, merges conservatively, and decays by calibrated half-life affected by
temperature/salinity. It does not stick in world screen space.

Foam lifecycle states are CrestWhitewater, AdvectedPatch, ThinFilm, ShoreDeposit,
and Dissolved. Breaker/wake energy creates coverage/thickness with a calibrated
J-to-m2 conversion and bounded entrained-air fraction. Conservative
semi-Lagrangian/BFECC advection carries cell/world position and generation;
compression/divergence changes thickness, diffusion spreads coverage,
coalescence preserves covered-volume proxy, and age/temperature/salinity/UV
drive decay. Shore contact either advects with swash, deposits a bounded beach
residue, or dissolves; wet/dry changes cannot leave foam over inland terrain.
Every transition updates EffectLedger.

Spray emission scales with breaker energy and wind shear. Particles integrate
gravity, drag, wind, collision with water/terrain, and evaporation at a visual
rate; water re-entry emits bounded ripple/aeration events but cannot recursively
amplify energy. Bubble size bands rise by buoyancy, advect/diffuse with flow,
dissolve, and alter optical scattering/acoustic noise. Rain impacts are
blue-noise distributed by physical precipitation flux. Mist is derived from
fine spray concentration.

Spray uses metres, m/s, kg-equivalent droplet mass, and joules; emitted kinetic
energy cannot exceed its event allocation. Bubble bands store air volume
fraction and radius metres; buoyant rise, pressure expansion, breakup/
coalescence, dissolution, and surface release conserve or explicitly transfer
air-volume proxy. Visual particles sample these fields but do not own them.
Recursive splash/re-entry emission has a gain below one and a depth/event
cooldown, guaranteeing termination.

Update rates, threading, and GPU ownership
-----------------------------------------

CPU event producers publish per ocean tick into bounded, sorted rings. Coarse
physical-source breaker/aeration summaries update at 15-30 Hz for audio/ecology
only. GPU foam/bubble tiles update at 15-30 Hz and spray each render frame on
async compute. EffectsSystem owns persistent pools/tiles; RenderGraph owns
transient lists, compaction, indirect arguments, and barriers. GPU summaries
are delayed telemetry and never affect vessel physics.

Lifecycle and dependencies
--------------------------

Initialize pools after ocean simulation and particle/material systems. Stream
effect tiles with ocean cells; on eviction, retain only meaningful coarse foam/
aeration state or account it as dissipated. Generation changes reproject valid
cell-anchored state or clear it with ledger accounting. Device loss discards
visual particles but preserves CPU source history sufficient for graceful
restart. See ..\rendering\28_particle_system.txt,
..\rendering\41_material_system.txt, 75_coastal_ocean_solver.txt,
77_ocean_interaction_wakes.txt, 78_ocean_surface_renderer.txt, and
79_underwater_media_renderer.txt.

Failure and error behavior
--------------------------

Errors include EventOverflow, StaleSourceGeneration, InvalidEnergy,
EffectPoolExhausted, TileUnavailable, FoamLedgerViolation,
AirVolumeViolation, RecursiveEmissionLimit, and GpuAllocationFailed. Overflow drops
lowest-importance visual events deterministically and increments ledger/
diagnostics; it never blocks the simulation thread. Missing effects do not alter
ocean shape. Non-finite events are quarantined by source ID. A pool failure
reduces spray first, then fine bubbles, while retaining coarse foam coverage.

Deterministic and testing strategy
----------------------------------

Event keys and counter-based random streams make emission independent of GPU
thread scheduling at aggregate level. Tests cover no-source decay, advection in
uniform current, foam mass bounds, breaker energy conversion, shore attachment,
vessel trail continuity, bubble rise/dissolution, rain flux, particle re-entry
non-amplification, origin shift, stream eviction, pool overflow ordering, and
save/load of coarse persistent state. GPU statistical tests compare distributions
and ledgers rather than requiring identical particle order.
Long-run tests measure foam half-life/coverage, zero-source monotonic decay,
wet/dry shoreline clearing, bubble air-volume closure, spray ballistic energy,
recursive gain, and identical aggregate ledgers across quality tiers.

Performance and scalability
---------------------------

Use sparse clipmapped tiles, append/compact buffers, indirect draws, occupancy
maps, event LOD, and fixed-capacity pools. Distant breakers render as aggregate
whitecap coverage; nearby spray/bubbles receive particles. Scale particle count,
tile resolution/update rate, bubble bands, and mist before foam extent/source
alignment. Profile events by source, alive/culled particles, tile occupancy,
compaction cost, overdraw, async overlap, pool pressure, and ledger residual.

Integration points
------------------

Deep, coastal, and interaction modules produce energy-tagged events. Surface
renderer consumes foam/whitewater and spray shadowing. Underwater media consumes
bubbles/sediment concentration. Habitat/ecosystem may consume bounded turbidity
or disturbance summaries, never individual visual particles. AudioSystem uses
the rate-limited CPU summary for surf, spray, rain, and bubbles.

Engine-wide integration contract
--------------------------------

Normative owner of visual effects plus bounded CPU presentation summaries, not
authoritative water/ecology. Producers publish energy/provenance events after
P9; GPU simulation/render runs P15 and cannot feed P2-P12. Audio/ecology consume
separate rate-limited CPU summaries, never GPU particles. Pools/histories retire
through GpuCompletionSet. VisualSeed is separate from authoritative streams.
See ..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt and ..\FRAME_THREADING_MODEL.txt.
