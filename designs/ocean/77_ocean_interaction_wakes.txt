OCEAN INTERACTION AND WAKES CLASS DESIGN
========================================

Class
-----

    OceanInteractionSystem

Purpose
-------

Couple vessel displacement, propulsors, rudders, impacts, and other bounded
water disturbances to deep and shallow ocean representations with conserved
volume/momentum and physically plausible Kelvin wakes, near fields, and
reaction forces.

Public API
----------

    OceanInteractorId Register(const OceanInteractorDescription&);
    void UpdateKinematics(OceanInteractorId, const InteractorKinematics&);
    void QueueImpulse(const WaterImpulse&);
    void FixedUpdate(OceanTick, FixedDeltaSeconds);
    InteractionQueryView AcquireQueryView() const;
    InteractionRenderWork BuildRenderWork(const OceanRenderSnapshot&) const;
    InteractionReaction ConsumeReaction(OceanInteractorId, OceanTick);
    void Unregister(OceanInteractorId);

Key data structures
-------------------

InteractorKinematics contains double pose, hull waterplane/displaced-volume
samples, linear/angular velocity, acceleration, draft, propulsor/rudder forces,
and vessel generation. PressureFootprint is a normalized moving pressure/
displacement distribution. WakePatch stores cell-anchored height/velocity
perturbation, spectrum or finite-volume fields, age, energy, and zero-mean
correction. WakePatch also stores stable-energy estimate, CFL/dispersion limit,
absorbing-boundary state, and last-valid generation. ConservativeSource records injected volume, momentum, impulse,
dissipation, equal/opposite reaction, and ForceOwner. ForceOwner is exactly one
of HydrodynamicsAlreadyApplied, OceanCouplingAppliesNextTick, PropulsorAlreadyApplied,
or ExternalWorld. InteractionQueryView contains compact
near-field kernels required by authoritative queries.

Algorithms and models
---------------------

Derive the pressure footprint from displaced volume and waterplane change,
rather than boat speed alone. Offshore, evolve a linear dispersive wake patch
in Fourier space under finite-depth dispersion, producing the correct
Froude/depth-dependent Kelvin pattern; add a bounded nonlinear near-field source
for stern/transom flow. In shallow tiles, deposit conservative mass/momentum
sources into CoastalOceanSolver so wakes refract, steepen, reflect, and run up.

Propulsors inject directed momentum/turbulence based on applied mechanical
force; reaction is already represented in vessel propulsion and is tagged to
avoid double application. Hull displacement changes exchange vertical momentum
and return hydrodynamic reaction only when ForceOwner is
OceanCouplingAppliesNextTick. Pressure/drag already applied by
HydrodynamicsSystem is
recorded for the ledger and never applied a second time. An impulse ledger
checks that water plus the single owned reaction balances external impulse
within tolerance.

The coupling is explicit in time: immutable ocean generation N drives vessel
physics N; post-step kinematics and sources are deposited while constructing
N+1; any ocean-owned reaction is tagged N+1. There is no recursive re-query or
mutation of generation N. A configurable predictor may estimate the N+1 hull
footprint, but its correction remains in the next tick and is conservation-
checked.
Wake energy dissipates through physical spreading, viscosity/turbulence, bottom
drag, and breaking; dissipated energy feeds effects, not arbitrary timed sine
amplitude.

Each source is normalized by numerical integration on its destination grid
before deposit. Time ramps bound start/stop jerk. Linear spectral patches use an
energy-stable analytic phase step plus sponge layers whose removed energy enters
the ledger. Nonlinear/coastal deposits obey the destination CFL and positivity
limits; excess source is substepped or retained in the pending ledger, never
clamped away. A wake with no source/current forcing may only conserve or lose
the modeled energy. Growth, aliasing above the patch Nyquist limit, reflection
from patch edges, or steepness beyond the breaking limit triggers subdivision,
dissipation, or quarantine rather than an unstable amplitude cap.

Overlapping far wake patches may merge only after conserving first moments,
momentum, and spectral energy bands. Near patches never merge across solver
regimes. A partition-of-unity window prevents double counting pressure near
field, spectral wake, and shallow-water source.

Update rates, threading, and GPU ownership
-----------------------------------------

Pre-step and post-step interactor kinematics latch on the two sides of the 60 Hz
physics/ocean coupling barrier. Authoritative near fields and shallow sources update at 60 Hz; far
offshore spectral patches may update at 15-30 Hz analytically. CPU state owns
forces and query data. GPU compute owns high-resolution visual wake patches and
foam advection, initialized from the same source ledger. GPU readback cannot
change reaction forces.

Lifecycle and dependencies
--------------------------

Registration validates hull descriptors and binds a generational EntityId.
During region transitions, a handoff transaction projects a wake between deep
and coastal forms while preserving volume/momentum, then retires the old patch.
Unregister stops sources but leaves detached wakes to decay/stream normally.
Cell unload serializes persistent energetic patches or conservatively exports
their boundary flux. See ..\sailing\25_vessel_system.txt,
..\sailing\49_hydrodynamics.txt, ..\simulation\24_physics_world.txt,
75_coastal_ocean_solver.txt, 76_ocean_query_service.txt, and
85_ocean_streaming_persistence.txt.

Failure and error behavior
--------------------------

Errors include InvalidHullFootprint, StaleInteractor, DuplicateTick,
SourceCapacity, CoastalTileUnavailable, ProjectionLossExceeded,
ImpulseImbalance, EnergyGrowth, WakeCflExceeded, PatchAliasing,
ForceOwnerConflict, and NonFiniteKinematics. Invalid interactor data disables only
that source and reports it to vessel diagnostics. If a visual wake allocation
fails, authoritative forces/sources continue. If a required shallow tile is
unavailable, cap the source and retain it in a bounded pending ledger; never
inject it offshore through land.

Deterministic and testing strategy
----------------------------------

Source IDs, cell keys, and deposits are sorted before fixed-tree accumulation.
Kernels and Fourier grids are versioned. Tests cover stationary displacement,
constant-speed Kelvin angle and wavelength, Froude/depth transitions, start/
stop transients, source integral, reaction sign, momentum/energy ledger,
force-owner exactly-once application, one-tick coupling schedule,
shallow refraction/reflection, two-wake superposition, patch merge, deep/coastal
handoff, unregister persistence, origin shift, save/load, and CPU/GPU source
initialization conformance.
Stability tests include impulsive start, maximum supported Froude number, patch
edge crossing, no-source 30-minute evolution, source substep/backlog, steepness
breaking, Nyquist rejection, and monotonic energy after forcing stops.

Performance and scalability
---------------------------

Allocate fixed wake patch pools by importance. Use sparse tiles, analytic far
wakes, FFT patch batching, conservative low-resolution CPU kernels, and GPU
texture arrays. Degrade remote visual resolution/update rate/foam before
authoritative near-vessel sources. Cap per-interactor deposition radius from
physical group velocity and age. Profile active patches, cells touched,
spectral updates, merge/projection loss, source backlog, and GPU milliseconds.

Integration points
------------------

VesselSystem and PhysicsWorld supply motion and consume tagged reaction.
HydrodynamicsSystem/propulsion supply force provenance. Coastal and deep modules
receive sources. QueryService composes perturbations. Effects consume
dissipation, vorticity, aeration, and breaker events. Audio consumes cavitation,
bow/stern, and impact events. Network/save replicate source seeds and persistent
state, not visual textures.

Engine-wide integration contract
--------------------------------

Normative wake/source owner. EntityId and OceanInteractorId are distinct typed
generations. P8 supplies post-physics values; P9 builds N+1; tagged reaction is
eligible only for next P6. ForceProvenance is the exactly-once cycle break.
Optional GPU wake generations never own physics and retire centrally through
GpuCompletionSet. See ..\FRAME_THREADING_MODEL.txt,
..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt, and ..\sailing\INDEX.txt.
