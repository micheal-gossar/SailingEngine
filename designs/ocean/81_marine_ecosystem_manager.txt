MARINE ECOSYSTEM MANAGER CLASS DESIGN
=====================================

Class
-----

    MarineEcosystemManager

Purpose
-------

Simulate a persistent, data-oriented marine ecosystem across the streamed world:
distant populations remain statistical and conservative while nearby cohorts
promote into full animal agents without creating or losing individuals.

Public API
----------

    Result<void, EcosystemError> LoadCatalog(const MarineSpeciesCatalog&);
    void SetObservers(span<const EcosystemObserver>);
    void ActivateCell(OceanCellKey, const HabitatSnapshot&);
    void DeactivateCell(OceanCellKey);
    void Advance(OceanTick);
    EcosystemView AcquireView() const;
    AgentPromotionBatch ConsumePromotions();
    void ReturnAgents(const AgentDemotionBatch&);
    EcosystemSaveDelta BuildSaveDelta(OceanCellKey) const;

Key data structures
-------------------

SpeciesDefinition stores stable id/version, trophic guild, size/age stages,
temperature/salinity/depth/oxygen tolerances, diel/seasonal migration, fecundity,
mortality, diet matrix, habitat affinities, social group rules, vessel response,
acoustic traits, visual asset set, and ethical interaction capabilities.
PopulationCohort is SoA: species/stage, abundance, biomass, mean/variance energy,
health, reproduction state, location distribution, genetic/seed token, and
provenance. EcosystemCell stores carrying capacities, primary productivity,
detritus/nutrients, habitat quality, fishing/disturbance pressure if gameplay
uses it, migration fluxes, and promoted-individual reservations.

Algorithms and models
---------------------

At distance, integrate stage-structured cohorts using bounded birth/death,
metabolism, predation functional responses, carrying capacity, seasonal/diel
migration, and advection by currents. Use conservative transfer ledgers for
abundance, biomass, and energy between cohorts/cells/trophic pools. Stochastic
events draw from counter-based distributions keyed by cell/species/time epoch;
they cannot depend on job completion order.

HabitatSystem owns physical patch area/health/suitability; EcosystemManager owns
food resources, realized carrying capacity, cohorts, and trophic transfers.
Capacity is computed in kg wet biomass and individual limits from usable patch
area, primary production, shelter, temperature/oxygen stress, and species
competition. It is a slowly varying bound, not an instant despawn target:
excess raises migration/mortality pressure over biological time. Food-web edges
transfer consumed biomass energy through species assimilation efficiency;
unassimilated energy enters detritus, respiration exits through a named sink,
and no predator can consume more available prey biomass than the ledger.

Migration separates intent, flux, and visualization. Seasonal/diel habitat
fields produce a rate-limited route/neighbor flux; current adds advection;
barriers/depth/temperature can close an edge. Loaded cells exchange one
canonical integer/stochastic abundance and biomass transfer. For unloaded
destinations, OceanStreamingService stores a migratory transit cohort keyed by
route and arrival tick. Promoted agents crossing a tier boundary reserve and
transfer the same identity; they are not simultaneously counted in both cells.

Promotion samples explicit nearby individuals/groups from a cohort's stored
distribution and subtracts an exact reservation from statistical abundance.
The reservation carries stable identity seeds, age/energy/health, group id, and
behavior state into MarineAgentSimulation. Demotion aggregates surviving agents
back into the original or migrated cell/cohort; deaths/births already recorded
by agents reconcile through the ledger. Hysteresis radii and cooldown prevent
tier thrashing. Whales/dolphins and other individually persistent species may
remain identity records globally while their motion is statistically advanced.

Predator/prey outcomes at statistical scale are rates; nearby outcomes are
behavioral state transitions and non-graphic despawn/escape/feeding events.
Technical states may include Avoid, Pursue, Feed, Injured, Dead, or Recovered,
but rendering/gameplay must follow configured non-graphic presentation.

Update rates, threading, and GPU ownership
-----------------------------------------

Distant cells advance at 0.2-1 Hz, important migratory/persistent cohorts at
1-2 Hz, promotion planning at 5 Hz, all from integer ocean ticks. Jobs operate
on cell-local next buffers; canonical edge flux and trophic ledgers reduce in a
stable sorted phase before publish. CPU state is authoritative and saved.
GPU receives compact density/biome fields for rendering only and never performs
population births, deaths, promotion, or save-state updates.

Lifecycle and dependencies
--------------------------

Load and version the species catalog before world activation. Streaming loads
base habitat plus saved deltas, warms neighboring migration boundaries, then
activates a cell on a tick boundary. Deactivation first requests agent demotion,
settles ledgers, serializes dirty state, and retains lightweight migratory flux
continuations. Catalog migration is explicit per species version.

Failure and error behavior
--------------------------

Errors include UnknownSpecies, CatalogVersionMismatch, InvalidHabitat,
NegativePopulation, LedgerImbalance, PromotionCapacity, MissingNeighborFlux,
CapacityOwnershipConflict, MigrationTransitMismatch, FoodWebEnergyViolation,
CorruptSaveDelta, and NonFiniteCohort. Validation rejects impossible rate or
diet matrices. A bad cell is quarantined at its last valid generation; migration
boundaries close conservatively. Promotion capacity pressure keeps abundance in
the cohort and reduces visible agents; it never silently deletes animals.
Catalog/save incompatibility invokes a named migration or preserves opaque data.

Deterministic and testing strategy
----------------------------------

Use fixed ticks, sorted species/cell keys, rational rate buckets, counter RNG,
and deterministic reductions. Tests cover zero-growth equilibrium, logistic
capacity, predator/prey stability bounds, seasonal/diel migration, current
advection, cell flux conservation, promotion/demotion round trip, observer
hysteresis, persistent cetacean identity, save/load and unloaded-time catch-up,
thread-count replay, catalog migration, population non-negativity, and
mass/energy ledger tolerances over long accelerated runs.
Acceptance also checks closed-food-web biomass/energy accounting, no prey
overdraw, capacity response without instant deletion, loaded/unloaded migration
equivalence, route barrier handling, and exact cell/promotion/transit identity
counts over seasonal replay.

Performance and scalability
---------------------------

Store cohorts in SoA pages, aggregate rare/remote stages, sleep steady cells,
use sparse neighbor flux, variable update frequency, and bounded catch-up
integration with analytic/implicit rate steps. Promotion budgets are per
species/importance and independent of statistical abundance. Profile active/
sleeping cells, cohorts, promoted reservations, solver time, ledger residual,
save bytes, migration traffic, and ecological error from each LOD tier.

Integration points
------------------

Environment forcing supplies currents, temperature, salinity, depth, tide, and
season. Habitat renderer/manager supplies coral, kelp, seagrass, reef, pelagic,
and seabed suitability. MarineAgentSimulation executes promoted animals.
UnderwaterAcoustics receives population and vocalization schedules. Renderer
receives bounded density fields and agents. Gameplay commands use explicit,
ethical interaction capabilities and update ledgers through validated events.

Engine-wide integration contract
--------------------------------

Normative population/cohort/trophic owner at P10. Habitat owns potential
physical patch inputs; ecosystem owns realized capacity/biomass/populations;
agents own promoted reservations exclusively. Their mutual-looking flow is
immutable snapshot then next-generation delta/ledger, not ownership. State uses
WorldPosition, TickId, AssetId/PersistentEntityId, SI, and counter-based
DeterministicStreamKey from ..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt. Save/streaming are
provider/participant interfaces.
