HYDRODYNAMICS CLASS DESIGN
==========================

Class:

    HydrodynamicsSystem

Purpose:

    Compute water drag, lift, planing, slamming, and keel forces from hull motion and ocean samples.

Public API:

    RegisterHull(vessel,hullDef);
    UnregisterHull(vessel);
    ComputeForces(vessel,bodyState,oceanSamples,FixedDeltaSeconds)->ForceSet;
    DebugForces(vessel);

Internal state and components:

    - hull panels/coefficients.
    - wetted-state history.
    - filtered velocities.
    - planing/slam state.
    - debug vectors.

Lifecycle and ownership:

    hull definitions immutable assets; runtime cache owned per vessel and removed before physics body

Error behavior:

    Invalid/stale hull, coefficients, units, query generation, non-finite state,
    or numerical-domain failure returns HydrodynamicsError with no partial force.

Threading and timing constraints:

    fixed simulation step; queries batched before force computation; equations avoid frame-rate dependence and clamp pathological values

Dependencies:

    - PhysicsBodySnapshot value.
    - OceanQueryService results.
    - AssetManager.

Tests:

    - zero relative velocity.
    - drag direction.
    - keel lift.
    - planing threshold.
    - slam impulse clamp.
    - dry hull.
    - coefficient validation.

Implementation rules:

    - Keep implementation-only types out of public headers.
    - Use RAII and generational IDs; do not expose owning raw pointers.
    - Validate descriptions before allocating or mutating state.
    - Publish complete snapshots/generations rather than partially updated data.
    - Add diagnostics for failure context without making logging a dependency
      of correctness.

Canonical integration contract:

    Normative stateless/P6 cached ForceSet producer. It does not call
    PhysicsWorld or VesselSystem; the coordinator supplies body state and one
    coherent OceanQueryResults batch, then applies the returned force/torque.
    Hull definitions are immutable AssetId generations. SI and ForceProvenance
    distinguish pressure/drag already applied from next-tick ocean reaction;
    buoyancy/hydrodynamic terms have a documented non-overlapping partition.

    See 07_buoyancy_system.txt, INDEX.txt,
    ..\ocean\76_ocean_query_service.txt, and
    ..\ocean\77_ocean_interaction_wakes.txt.
