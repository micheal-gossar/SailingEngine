VESSEL SYSTEM CLASS DESIGN
==========================

Class:

    VesselSystem

Purpose:

    Compose hull, sails, rudder, buoyancy, propulsion, damage, and control into fixed-step vessel simulation.

Public API:

    Spawn(VesselDefinition,Transform)->VesselId;
    Despawn(id);
    SetControls(id,VesselControls);
    Step(context);
    State(id) const;
    ApplyDamage(...);
    AttachMooring(...);

Internal state and components:

    - entity/body mappings.
    - component arrays for hull/sails/rudder/keel/crew/damage.
    - control state.
    - cached ocean/wind samples.

Lifecycle and ownership:

    EntityRegistry owns EntityId; VesselSystem owns VesselId mappings, runtime
    sailing records, and orchestration. PhysicsWorld owns BodyId; force
    subsystems own registered caches; AssetManager owns retained definitions.
    Despawn removes constraints/body and subsystem registrations before mappings.

Error behavior:

    Spawn/control/step/despawn return VesselError for invalid/stale IDs,
    definition/dependency, phase, capacity, or force/query failure. Spawn and
    despawn are transactional; partial mappings/bodies/registrations roll back.

Threading and timing constraints:

    Simulation-thread P5/P6/P8 only. Stable order is command/control latch,
    coherent weather/ocean sample batch, pending mass change, force producers,
    P7 PhysicsWorld integration, then post-step kinematics/damage/events.

Dependencies:

    - PhysicsWorld.
    - BuoyancySystem.
    - SailAerodynamicsSystem.
    - HydrodynamicsSystem.
    - RudderSteeringSystem.
    - WeatherSnapshot and OceanQueryService values.

Tests:

    - spawn/despawn.
    - zero wind.
    - sail/rudder force routing.
    - capsize.
    - damaged component.
    - deterministic subsystem ordering.

Implementation rules:

    - Keep implementation-only types out of public headers.
    - Use RAII and generational IDs; do not expose owning raw pointers.
    - Validate descriptions before allocating or mutating state.
    - Publish complete snapshots/generations rather than partially updated data.
    - Add diagnostics for failure context without making logging a dependency
      of correctness.

Canonical integration contract:

    Normative sailing coordinator. Subsystems consume VesselStepContext and
    return ForceSet/events; they do not depend on or recursively call
    VesselSystem. Every force/reaction has exactly one ForceProvenance owner.
    Ocean generation N drives P6; post-step OceanCouplingBatch builds N+1 and
    any ocean-owned reaction applies next tick. Vessel controls enter only
    through GameplayCommandBus.

    See INDEX.txt, ..\FRAME_THREADING_MODEL.txt,
    ..\ocean\72_ocean_domain_coordinator.txt, and
    ..\ocean\77_ocean_interaction_wakes.txt.
