SAIL AERODYNAMICS CLASS DESIGN
==============================

Class:

    SailAerodynamicsSystem

Purpose:

    Convert apparent wind, sail trim, sail geometry, and aerodynamic curves
    into forces and torques applied to vessel rigid bodies.

Public API:

    void RegisterSail(EntityId vessel, SailId sail, const SailDescription& description);
    void SetTrim(EntityId vessel, SailId sail, float normalizedTrim);
    void SetRaisedFraction(EntityId vessel, SailId sail, float fraction);
    void FixedUpdate(
        PhysicsWorld& physics,
        const WeatherSnapshot& weather,
        FixedDeltaSeconds fixedDelta);
    SailTelemetry GetTelemetry(EntityId vessel, SailId sail) const;

SailDescription:

    Area.
    Local center of effort.
    Rest orientation.
    Minimum/maximum trim angle.
    Lift coefficient curve.
    Drag coefficient curve.
    Stall angle.
    Rig type.

Calculations:

    apparentWind = trueWind - vesselPointVelocity
    transform apparent wind to sail-local space
    calculate angle of attack
    calculate dynamic pressure
    lookup lift and drag coefficients
    calculate force directions and magnitudes
    apply force at center of effort

Behavior:

    Smooth trim actuator movement.
    Reduce force by raised/reefed fraction.
    Model luffing at ineffective angles.
    Model stall at high angles of attack.
    Export tension/luffing data to animation and audio.

Rules:

    Force curves are data assets, not hardcoded per ship.
    Curves are continuous at sample boundaries.
    External normalized trim is validated by command ingestion; a bounded
    actuator clamps only its documented physical range and reports saturation.
    FixedUpdate performs no allocations.
    Damage modifies effective area through explicit vessel state.

Tests:

    Zero apparent wind produces zero force.
    Force scales with velocity squared.
    Correct force direction on port/starboard tack.
    Stall reduces lift.
    Reefing reduces total force.
    Center-of-effort offset produces torque.
    Deterministic fixed-step output.

Dependencies:

    WeatherSnapshot
    PhysicsWorld
    AssetManager

Canonical integration contract:

    Normative P6 ForceSet producer, not a VesselSystem owner. VesselSystem passes
    a VesselStepContext/body state and applies the returned newton/metre torque
    once. True/apparent wind use m/s toward-motion in root axes; imported
    meteorological "from" converts in WeatherSystem. SailId is a typed
    generation handle scoped to EntityId. Curves are retained AssetId
    generations and immutable during a tick.

    Invalid/stale registration, curve, units, or non-finite context returns
    SailError; no exceptions or silent zero-force fallback. See
    ..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt, ..\world\09_weather_system.txt, and INDEX.txt.
