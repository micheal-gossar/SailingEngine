SIMULATION CLOCK CLASS DESIGN
=============================

Class:

    SimulationClock

Purpose:

    Convert real elapsed time into bounded fixed simulation steps and a render interpolation factor.

Public API:

    Reset(MonotonicTimePoint now);
    Advance(MonotonicTimePoint now)->Result<StepPlan,ClockError>;
    FixedDelta() const->FixedDeltaSeconds;
    Tick() const->TickId;
    Alpha() const->RenderInterpolationAlpha;
    Pause();
    Resume();
    SetTimeScale(scale);
    ConsumeStep();

Internal state and components:

    - last timestamp.
    - accumulator.
    - fixed delta.
    - tick index.
    - max catch-up steps.
    - pause/time scale.
    - dropped-time counter.

Lifecycle and ownership:

    ApplicationHost owns the clock. StepPlan is a value consumed once in P1;
    TickId is monotonically increasing. The rational fixed period is immutable
    while a world is active and is stored in save/network/ocean epochs.

Error behavior:

    Invalid period, time scale, non-monotonic input, or illegal lifecycle returns
    ClockError. A large elapsed interval is bounded by explicit catch-up/drop
    policy and reports dropped duration; it is not silently consumed.

Threading and timing constraints:

    Main loop only; fixed period immutable while world active; clamp huge
    elapsed time; RenderInterpolationAlpha is in [0,1).

Dependencies:

    - Monotonic platform clock value.
    - DebugDiagnostics.

Tests:

    - steady cadence.
    - jitter accumulation.
    - max catch-up clamp.
    - pause/resume no spike.
    - time scale.
    - alpha.
    - monotonic tick.

Implementation rules:

    - Keep implementation-only types out of public headers.
    - Use RAII and generational IDs; do not expose owning raw pointers.
    - Validate descriptions before allocating or mutating state.
    - Publish complete snapshots/generations rather than partially updated data.
    - Add diagnostics for failure context without making logging a dependency
      of correctness.

Canonical integration contract:

    Normative time authority. FixedDelta drives P2-P12 only.
    RenderInterpolationAlpha is clamped to [0,1) and drives P14 only. Render or
    audio callback delta never advances physics, gameplay, weather, ocean,
    persistence, or network state.

    See ..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt, ..\FRAME_THREADING_MODEL.txt, and
    ..\INTEGRATION_ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.txt.
