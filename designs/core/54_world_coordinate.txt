WORLD COORDINATE CLASS DESIGN
=============================

Class:

    WorldCoordinateSystem

Purpose:

    Centralize double-precision world coordinates, floating-origin conversion, cell keys, and coordinated origin shifts.

Public API:

    ToRender(WorldPosition,RenderOrigin)->Result<RenderPosition,CoordinateError>;
    ToWorld(RenderPosition,RenderOrigin)->Result<WorldPosition,CoordinateError>;
    Canonicalize(WorldPosition)->Result<WorldPosition,CoordinateError>;
    RequestRebase(WorldPosition observer);
    PreparePendingRebase()->RebaseTransaction;
    Commit(RebaseTransaction,span<RebaseParticipant>);
    Origin() const->RenderOrigin;

Internal state and components:

    - double origin.
    - cell size.
    - pending shift.
    - origin generation.
    - registered shift participants.
    - conversion bounds.

Lifecycle and ownership:

    World owns the coordinate service. Participants register RAII tokens; shift
    prepare validates all participants and commit publishes one
    OriginGeneration. Authoritative cell/local positions are not rewritten.

Error behavior:

    Non-finite/out-of-range/non-canonical coordinates, stale origin generation,
    or participant prepare failure return CoordinateError before commit.

Threading and timing constraints:

    rebase only at simulation/frame safe point with GPU idle not required when snapshots are generation-tagged; render packets carry generation

Dependencies:

    - None at ownership level.
    - Physics, terrain, ocean, camera, audio, and rendering register
      RebaseParticipant interfaces and consume immutable RenderOrigin values.

Tests:

    - round-trip precision.
    - negative cells.
    - threshold.
    - participant ordering.
    - generation mismatch.
    - physics/terrain alignment.

Implementation rules:

    - Keep implementation-only types out of public headers.
    - Use RAII and generational IDs; do not expose owning raw pointers.
    - Validate descriptions before allocating or mutating state.
    - Publish complete snapshots/generations rather than partially updated data.
    - Add diagnostics for failure context without making logging a dependency
      of correctness.

Canonical integration contract:

    Normative coordinate authority. WorldPosition uses signed WorldCellKey plus
    double local metres, +X east/+Y north/+Z up. Render positions are float
    offsets from a double high/low origin and always carry OriginGeneration.
    Rebase commits at P3; GPU idle is not required because in-flight packets
    retain the old generation through their GpuCompletionSet.

    See ..\CANONICAL_CONVENTIONS.txt, ..\FRAME_THREADING_MODEL.txt, and
    ..\ocean\87_ocean_conventions_invariants.txt.
